Uplight Clip 2

Space-saving, intelligent lighting—clamped directly to your workspace.

The UPLIGHT Clip 2 delivers all the intelligent lighting features of our floor-standing models in a sleek, desk-mounted form. Designed for compact or minimalist setups, this glare-free luminaire clips directly to your desk—saving space without compromising on performance.

Equipped with advanced daylight, motion, and proximity sensors, the Clip.2 automatically adapts brightness and color temperature to your environment and presence. With ultra-low glare (UGR <6) and adjustable light distribution, it ensures visual comfort throughout your day.

The swiveling and telescoping head, stepless dimming, and four selectable color temperatures make it a highly customizable tool for focused, ergonomic lighting. Built with durable Philips LEDs, flicker-free operation, and a long lifespan, the UPLIGHT Clip.2 brings efficiency and smart functionality to any modern desk space.

Key Features:

  • Space-saving clip-on design — mounts directly to your desk
  • Ultra low-glare with UGR <6 for optimal visual comfort
  • Swiveling and telescopic lamp head for precise adjustability
  • Four selectable color temperatures: 3000K / 4000K / 5000K / 6000K
  • Independent control of direct (70%) and indirect (30%) light output
  • Beam angle: 110° (direct) / 50° (indirect)
  • Stepless dimming via intuitive touch control
  • Intelligent sensors: daylight, motion, and proximity detection
  • Luminous flux: 11,200 lm
  • LED chips: Philips SMD2835
  • Color Rendering Index (CRI): >80
  • Long lifespan: L80 (B10) 50,000 hours
  • Power consumption: 80W (active), <0.5W (standby)
  • MacAdam step: <3 SDCM
  • Flicker-free operation with Boke driver
  • Digital clock display on lamp head
  • Energy efficiency class: D
  • IP protection rating: IP20
  • Material: Aluminum housing
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 852 x 1310 x 620 mm
  • Cable length: 3 m
  • Available colors: Signal White, White Aluminum, Anthracite, Jet Black
  • Warranty: 36 months
