Smart Light

Essential lighting for modern offices — simple, elegant, and efficient.

The Smart Light offers a cost-effective lighting solution without compromising on style or performance. With its clean, minimal design and professional appearance, it integrates seamlessly into any office environment.

Delivering balanced direct and indirect illumination (70/30%), it provides a glare-free workspace experience, supporting comfortable and focused work throughout the day. The 4000K neutral white light ensures clarity without strain, while the flicker-free driver guarantees stable, reliable performance.

Built to last, the Smart Light features high-efficiency SMD2835 LEDs with a lifespan of 50,000 hours (L80/B10). With its DALI and push dimming compatibility, you can easily adjust the light to suit your needs — making it a versatile, durable, and affordable choice for any modern workplace.

Key Features:

  • Glare-free office lighting with UGR <19
  • Balanced 70/30% direct/indirect light distribution
  • Neutral white color temperature (CCT: 4000K)
  • Luminous flux: 10,400 lm (7,400 lm ↑ / 3,000 lm ↓)
  • High efficiency: 130 lm/W
  • Long lifespan: L80 (B10) 50,000 hours
  • High color rendering: CRI >80
  • Beam angle: 90° (direct) / 110° (indirect)
  • Dimming: DALI and Push dimming support
  • Flicker-free driver: Boke
  • LED type: SMD2835
  • Input power: 80W total (50W ↑ / 30W ↓)
  • Power factor: ≥0.9
  • Input voltage: AC 200–240V
  • IP20 protection rating
  • Available colors: Black, Anthracite, Grey, White
