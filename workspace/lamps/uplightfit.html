Uplight Fit.2

Intelligent, glare-free lighting—directly integrated into your desk.

The UPLIGHT Fit.2 brings advanced lighting technology directly to your desktop with a clean, integrated design. Ideal for modern office environments, it combines powerful performance with space-efficient installation—mounting seamlessly into your desk surface for a minimalist, clutter-free setup.

With intelligent daylight, motion, and proximity sensors, the Fit.2 automatically adapts to your presence and ambient conditions. Its ultra-low glare (UGR <6) protects your eyes from strain, while customizable light distribution and color temperature support your well-being and focus throughout the day.

Designed with a swiveling and telescopic lamp head, stepless dimming, and Philips SMD2835 LED technology, the UPLIGHT Fit.2 offers powerful, ergonomic illumination that adjusts to your needs. Built for reliability, efficiency, and modern workspaces, it’s an intelligent choice for those who expect more from their lighting.

Key Features:

  • Desk-integrated design for a seamless, space-saving lighting solution
  • Ultra low-glare with UGR <6 for exceptional visual comfort
  • Swiveling and telescopic lamp head for precise adjustability
  • Four selectable color temperatures: 3000K / 4000K / 5000K / 6000K
  • Independent control of direct (70%) and indirect (30%) light output
  • Beam angle: 110° (direct) / 50° (indirect)
  • Stepless dimming via intuitive touch control
  • Intelligent sensors: daylight, motion, and proximity detection
  • Luminous flux: 11,200 lm
  • LED chips: Philips SMD2835
  • Color Rendering Index (CRI): >80
  • Long lifespan: L80 (B10) 50,000 hours
  • Power consumption: 80W (active), <0.5W (standby)
  • MacAdam step: <3 SDCM
  • Flicker-free operation with Boke driver
  • Digital clock display on lamp head
  • Energy efficiency class: D
  • IP protection rating: IP20
  • Material: Aluminum housing
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 852 x 1310 x 620 mm
  • Cable length: 3 m
  • Available colors: Signal White, White Aluminum, Anthracite, Jet Black
  • Warranty: 36 months
