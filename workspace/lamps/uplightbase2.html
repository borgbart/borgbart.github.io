Uplight Base 2

Intelligent, ergonomic lighting for enhanced focus and workplace comfort.

Experience the innovative UPLIGHT Base 2 Floor Light - a perfect fusion of intelligent technology and modern design, crafted to elevate your work environment and boost productivity.

This advanced office light uses integrated sensors to respond to your presence and the ambient light conditions, adjusting brightness and color temperature automatically. Its ultra-low glare (UGR <6) ensures exceptional visual comfort, reducing eye strain even during long hours of focused work.

With a swiveling and telescopic lamp head, stepless dimming via touch, and customizable light distribution, the UPLIGHT One.2 adapts perfectly to your workspace needs. Built with high-efficiency Philips LEDs and durable components, it offers a long service life, high performance, and professional aesthetics in one intelligent lighting solution.

Key Features:

  • Ultra glare-free with UGR <6
  • Four selectable color temperatures: 3000K / 4000K / 5000K / 6000K
  • Intelligent sensors: Daylight sensor (for color temp), motion sensor, and distance sensor
  • Swivel and telescopic lamp head for optimal adjustability
  • Independently controllable light distribution: 70% direct / 30% indirect
  • Stepless dimming via intuitive touch control
  • Luminous flux: 11,200 lm
  • Beam angle: 110° (direct) / 50° (indirect)
  • LED chips: Philips SMD2835
  • Color Rendering Index (CRI): >80
  • Long lifespan: L80 (B10) 50,000 hours
  • Input power: 80W total
  • Standby power consumption: <0.5W
  • MacAdam step: <3 SDCM
  • Digital clock display for added functionality
  • Flicker-free operation with Boke driver
  • Energy efficiency class: D
  • IP20 protection rating
  • Material: Aluminum housing
  • Dimensions (W x H x D): 852 x 2011 x 620 mm
  • Cable length: 3 m
  • Available colors: Signal White, White Aluminum, Anthracite, Jet Black
