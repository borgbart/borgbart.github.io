E-POINT 4M3S Electric Height-Adjustable Conference Table Frame

Create an inspiring and dynamic atmosphere in your meeting and conference rooms with the E-POINT 4M3S electric height-adjustable table frame. Designed for large tabletops, it accommodates sizes from 800x1050 mm up to 2000x2000 mm, seamlessly integrating into any environment.

Safety is enhanced with the integrated double collision protection system, adjustable across eight sensitivity levels, and a built-in child lock. The memory function allows you to save your favorite heights, while optional USB-A charging ports keep your devices powered throughout your meetings.

Constructed from high-quality steel and finished in RAL 9017 Traffic Black, the E-POINT 4M3S offers a reliable dynamic load capacity of 150 kg, providing stability and durability for all conference and bench setups.

Key Features:

  • Electrically height-adjustable frame (600-1250 mm)
  • Four motors for smooth lifting
  • Three-segment lifting columns
  • Three programmable memory keys
  • Integrated double collision protection (8 sensitivity levels)
  • Quiet operation (<50 dB)
  • Supports table tops from 800x1050 mm to 2000x2000 mm
  • Designed for conference and bench use
  • Built-in child lock for extra safety
  • Available in Traffic Black
