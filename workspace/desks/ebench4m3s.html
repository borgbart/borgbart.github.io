E-BENCH 4M3S Electric Height-Adjustable Bench Frame

Experience flexibility and functionality in your workspace with the E-BENCH 4M3S. This electrically height-adjustable double table frame is specifically designed for versatile bench setups, allowing simultaneous height adjustments on both sides to accommodate multiple users with ease.

Compatible with a wide range of tabletop sizes, from 2x600x1050 mm up to 2x1000x2000 mm, the frame fits seamlessly into various spaces. Each side supports a robust dynamic load of 80 kg, ensuring stability and reliability throughout your workday.

The frame features USB-A charging ports and three programmable memory slots for effortless height adjustments, while the integrated double collision protection system, adjustable across eight sensitivity levels, guarantees safe operation. Additionally, the built-in child lock provides extra security against unintended use. Constructed from durable metal and finished in RAL 9017 Traffic Black, the E-BENCH 4M3S is built for long-term performance and professional workspaces.

Key Features:

  • Electrically height-adjustable double bench frame (600-1250 mm)
  • Four motors for smooth and stable lifting
  • Three columns for dual-user support
  • USB-A charging ports for convenience
  • Three programmable memory slots
  • Integrated double collision protection (8 sensitivity levels)
  • Can be extended on various sides for flexible setups
  • Quiet operation (<50 dB)
  • Supports dual table tops from 2x600x1050 mm to 2x1000x2000 mm
  • Available in Traffic Black, White Aluminium, and Pure White
