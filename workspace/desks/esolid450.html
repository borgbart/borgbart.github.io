E-SOLID 450 Heavy-Duty Table Frame

Experience the ultimate in ergonomic design and heavy-duty performance with the E-SOLID 450 table frame. This powerful, electrically height-adjustable frame is engineered for demanding professional environments, supporting an impressive load capacity of up to 450 kg. Its telescopic width range accommodates table tops from 700x1050 mm up to 1000x2000 mm, providing exceptional versatility for any workspace.

With a stroke range of 660 mm to 1120 mm, the E-SOLID 450 ensures smooth and reliable movement for users of all sizes. The four-motor system delivers strong, stable lifting, while the intuitive handset with LED display allows for effortless height adjustments. Three programmable memory settings make it easy to switch to your preferred heights instantly. Integrated double collision protection, adjustable across eight sensitivity levels, guarantees safe operation at all times.

Constructed from high-quality steel and finished in RAL 9006 White Aluminium, this heavy-duty frame combines durability with a sleek, professional look, making it the ideal choice for demanding workspaces.

Key Features:

  • Electrically height-adjustable heavy-duty frame (660-1120 mm)
  • Four motors for strong, stable lifting
  • Handset with LED display for precise adjustments
  • 3 programmable memory settings
  • Load capacity: 450 kg
  • 2-segment lifting columns for smooth operation
  • Telescopic frame width—fits table tops 700x1050 mm to 1000x2000 mm
  • Integrated double collision protection (8 sensitivity levels)
  • Available in Traffic Black, White Aluminium, and Pure White
  • Heavy-duty system for professional environments
