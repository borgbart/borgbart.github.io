VIMO Monitor Arm

The VIMO One is an ergonomic monitor arm designed to enhance workspace comfort and improve posture. It supports monitors up to 32 inches and weights up to 7 kg, making it ideal for both office and home setups.

Compatible with VESA mount standards 75x75 mm and 100x100 mm, installation is quick and easy. The adjustable design allows you to position your monitor at the optimal height, angle, and distance, reducing strain and enhancing productivity.

Available in sleek black and grey finishes, the VIMO One combines functionality with style, complementing any modern workstation.

Key Features:

  • Ergonomic monitor arm for improved workspace comfort
  • Supports monitors up to 32 inches
  • Maximum load: 7 kg
  • Compatible with VESA mounts: 75x75 mm and 100x100 mm
  • Width: 370 mm, Height: 75 mm, Depth: 190 mm
  • Designed for tabletop installation
  • Available colours: Black, Grey
  • Adjustable for optimal monitor height, angle, and distance
