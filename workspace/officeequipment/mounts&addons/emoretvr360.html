E-MORE TV Lift R360

The E-MORE TV Lift R360 is an advanced, electrically height-adjustable TV mount designed to enhance your viewing experience. With effortless height adjustment via remote control, it allows you to position your TV precisely where you want it.

Constructed from robust steel and finished in traffic black (RAL 9017), the lift supports TVs weighing up to 80 kg, with a stroke range from 860 mm to 1,860 mm. Its 360° swivel function ensures optimal viewing angles from any position in the room. The lift operates efficiently with automatic energy-saving features and a speed of 25 mm/s.

Key Features:

  • Electric height-adjustable TV lift
  • Supports dynamic load up to 80 kg
  • Stroke range: 860-1860 mm
  • VESA mount compatible: 600 x 400 mm
  • Input voltage: 110-240 V, Output voltage: 24 V
  • Efficiency: 25% max, 5 min on / 18 min off, automatic switch-off
  • Speed: 25 mm/s
  • TV size compatibility: 32-75 inch
  • 360° swivel for optimal viewing angles
  • Finish: Traffic black (RAL 9017)
