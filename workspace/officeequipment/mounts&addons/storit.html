STORIT Drawer

The STORIT drawer is a versatile, lockable storage solution designed to be compatible with all our table models. Easily attachable to the underside of your tabletop, it provides a secure space for your essentials.

Constructed from durable powder-coated steel, the drawer features a sophisticated anthracite felt insert for an elegant touch. It comes with an integrated lock and a complete key set, ensuring your belongings are safely stored.

Designed for quick and simple installation, the under-mounted drawer includes full-extension ball bearing slides for smooth and effortless operation.

Key Features:

  • Lockable under-mounted drawer for secure storage
  • Compatible with all table models
  • Durable powder-coated steel construction
  • Felt insert in anthracite for an elegant touch
  • Width: 400 mm, Height: 75 mm, Depth: 270 mm, Material thickness: 1.2 mm
  • Integrated lock with complete key set
  • Full-extension ball bearing slides for smooth operation
  • Available colours: Black, Anthracite, Grey, White
