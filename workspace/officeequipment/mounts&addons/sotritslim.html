STORIT Slim Drawer

The STORIT Slim drawer is a sleek, lockable storage solution designed to be mounted under the tabletop. Its slim profile provides practical storage without taking up valuable workspace, making it ideal for modern workstations.

Constructed from durable PVC, the drawer features a smooth inlay and a ball bearing runner for effortless opening and closing. The STORIT Slim is lockable using a BMB interchangeable cylinder, ensuring your items are securely stored. Quick and easy mounting under the tabletop allows for flexible installation.

Key Features:

  • Slim, lockable under-mounted drawer for secure storage
  • Durable PVC construction with smooth inlay
  • Drawer with ball bearing runner for smooth operation
  • Quick and easy mounting under the tabletop
  • Lockable with BMB interchangeable cylinder
  • Width: 718 mm, Height: 60 mm, Depth: 230 mm
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
  • Ideal for workspace organization without taking up space
