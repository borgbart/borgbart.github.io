Flexit M Cable Guide

The Flexit M cable management system demonstrates how efficient and flexible cable management can be for stand/sit workspaces. Its mesh construction provides excellent adaptability, making it ideal for use with height-adjustable desks.

A zip fastener runs along the entire length of the guide, allowing easy insertion and routing of cables. This design also provides the option to integrate an additional cable outlet anywhere along the system, ensuring tidy and organized workspaces.

Key Features:

  • Flexible cable management system for height-adjustable desks
  • Round mesh design for adaptability
  • Zip fastener along entire length for easy cable routing
  • Option to integrate additional cable outlets
  • Width: 132 mm, Height: 100 mm, Depth: 105 mm
  • Durable plastic construction
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
  • Ideal for keeping cables tidy and accessible in dynamic workspaces
