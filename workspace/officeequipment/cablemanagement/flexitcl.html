Flexit CL Cable Guide

The Flexit CL is a vertical cable guide designed with an accordion principle, providing a flexible and efficient solution for height-adjustable desks. Its innovative design compresses and expands, adapting to changing desk heights up to a maximum of 1,300 mm.

The wide, round cable guide accommodates multiple cables at once, keeping them neatly organized and out of the way. Constructed from durable PVC, the Flexit CL is available in four colours, allowing it to seamlessly match different office or home interiors.

Key Features:

  • Vertical accordion-style cable guide for height-adjustable desks
  • Expands and compresses to adapt to desk heights up to 1,300 mm
  • Wide, round guide accommodates multiple cables
  • Durable PVC construction
  • Round shape for smooth cable routing
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White, Anthracite
  • Ideal for stand/sit workspaces
  • Keeps cables tidy, organized, and out of the way
