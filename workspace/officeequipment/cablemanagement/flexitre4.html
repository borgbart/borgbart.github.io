Flexit RE4 Cable Guide

The Flexit RE4 cable guide is designed as the perfect complement to our electrically height-adjustable table frames, such as the E-MORE series. Ideal for stand/sit workspaces, it keeps cables neatly organized and safely out of the way, even in frequently adjusted desk setups.

Constructed from durable plastic, the RE4 features a flexible design with four separate chambers to accommodate a large number of cables. Its modular chain design allows for height adjustment by individually removing chain links, ensuring a perfect fit for your workspace. With this cable guide, you will never stumble over unorganized cables again.

Key Features:

  • Flexible cable guide for height-adjustable desks
  • Four chambers for holding multiple cables
  • Adjustable height by removing chain links individually
  • Durable plastic construction
  • Rectangular shape, Height: 1285 mm
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
  • Ideal for stand/sit workspaces and E-MORE table frames
  • Keeps cables organized and safely out of the way
