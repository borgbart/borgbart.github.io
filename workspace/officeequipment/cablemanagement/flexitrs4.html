Flexit RS4 Cable Guide

The Flexit RS4 is a 4-chamber cable guide designed for stand/sit workspaces, perfectly complementing electrically height-adjustable desks. It keeps cables organized and safe, moving seamlessly with the desk as it adjusts height.

Constructed from durable plastic, the RS4 consists of 36 chain links providing a maximum height of 1,350 mm. Each chamber allows for neat separation of multiple cables, and the total length can be easily adjusted by removing individual chain links. Optional magnetic holders and a secure screw mount allow flexible attachment to the desk.

This intelligent cable guide ensures that cables always stay tidy and in place, regardless of desk position, providing a reliable and professional cable management solution.

Key Features:

  • 4-chamber cable guide for organized cable routing
  • Adjustable height by removing individual chain links
  • Maximum height: 1,350 mm, Width: 110 mm, Depth: 46 mm
  • Durable plastic construction
  • Optional magnetic holders and secure screw mount
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
  • Ideal for stand/sit workspaces and height-adjustable desks
  • Keeps cables tidy and in place during desk movement
