Flexit RE Cable Guide

The Flexit RE is a flexible cable guide designed as the perfect complement to our electrically height-adjustable table frames, such as the E-MORE series. Ideal for stand/sit workspaces, it keeps cables neatly organized and safely out of the way, even with frequent height adjustments.

Constructed from durable plastic, the RE features two chambers to accommodate as many cables as possible. The guide can be mounted to the table frame using a magnet, allowing it to move with the desk while remaining securely in place. This ensures your cables stay tidy and well-managed across all desk heights.

Key Features:

  • Flexible 2-chamber cable guide for organized cable routing
  • Adjustable height by removing individual chain links
  • Width: 330 mm, Height: 1,320 mm, Depth: 60 mm
  • Durable plastic construction
  • Magnetic mounting for secure attachment to table frame
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
  • Ideal for stand/sit workspaces and E-MORE table frames
  • Keeps cables tidy and in place during desk movement
