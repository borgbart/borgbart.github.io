Flexit RT2 Cable Guide

The Flexit RT2 is a 2-chamber cable guide designed for stand/sit workspaces, providing organized cable management that moves seamlessly with height-adjustable desks. Its compact design ensures cables stay neatly routed and out of the way.

Constructed from durable polypropylene (PP) plastic, the RT2 consists of 45 chain links, providing a maximum height of 1,350 mm. Each chamber allows for separate cable routing, and the total length can be easily adjusted by removing individual chain links. Optional magnetic holders and a secure screw mount enable flexible and secure attachment to the desk.

This practical cable guide ensures your cables remain tidy and in place, even in frequently adjusted workspaces, making it an efficient solution for modern office environments.

Key Features:

  • 2-chamber cable guide for organized cable routing
  • Adjustable height by removing individual chain links
  • Maximum height: 1,350 mm
  • Durable polypropylene (PP) plastic construction
  • Optional magnetic holders and secure screw mount
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
  • Ideal for stand/sit workspaces and height-adjustable desks
  • Keeps cables tidy and securely in place during desk movement
