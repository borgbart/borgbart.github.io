Boxit E-MORE Cable Canal

The Boxit E-MORE cable canal is an elegant and practical solution for under-desk cable management. Designed to fit directly on the beams, it maximizes workspace efficiency by utilizing every available space without compromise.

Constructed from high-quality steel, the Boxit E-MORE combines durability with a sleek design, making it a perfect complement to modern workstations and office setups.

Key Features:

  • Elegant and practical cable canal for tidy cable management
  • Fits directly on beams—no space wasted
  • High-quality steel construction for durability
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
