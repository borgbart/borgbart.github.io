Varioplex Cable Tray

The Varioplex cable tray is a durable and adaptable cable management solution designed to maintain an organized and efficient workspace. Constructed from powder-coated steel, it delivers exceptional strength and a refined appearance, making it ideal for both office and industrial environments.

With an extendable length ranging from 1100 mm to 1700 mm, the tray can be easily adjusted to fit various desk or workstation widths. It includes two mounting brackets for straightforward installation, while the vertical cable guide can also be mounted when rotated 90°, offering flexible configuration options.

Key Features:

  • Sturdy powder-coated steel construction
  • Extendable length: 1100–1700 mm
  • Height: 77 mm, Depth: 117 mm, Material thickness: 1 mm
  • Includes 2 mounting brackets
  • Vertical cable guide mountable at 90° rotation
  • Mounting bracket hole distance: 77 mm
  • Available colours: Black, Anthracite, Grey, White
  • System: Varioplex
