Boxit Smart Detachable Cable Tray

The Boxit Smart detachable cable tray is a simple, practical, and cost-effective solution for under-desk cable management. Designed for quick and easy loading or unloading, the tray can be detached from one side, providing convenient access to your cables whenever needed.

Equipped with fixation holes for the multifunctional power strip ALUPOWER plus, it allows for a secure and organized cable setup. Made from durable powder-coated steel, the tray comes with two clamps included for easy installation.

Key Features:

  • Simple, practical, and cost-effective cable management
  • Detachable from one side for quick access
  • Fixation holes for ALUPOWER plus multifunctional power strip
  • Durable powder-coated steel construction
  • Comes with 2 clamps for easy installation
  • Available colours: Black, Anthracite, Grey, White
