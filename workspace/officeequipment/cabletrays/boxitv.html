Boxit V Cable Tray

The Compact cable tray is a robust and versatile solution for managing cables under desks and workstations. Made from high-quality steel, it combines durability with functional design, ensuring a tidy and efficient workspace.

Its large cable holes provide flexible routing options, allowing multiple cables to pass through neatly. The tray can be securely fixed using screws beneath the desktop, making installation quick and reliable.

Key Features:

  • Durable steel construction
  • Flexible cable routing with large cable holes
  • Height: 89 mm, Depth: 120 mm, Material thickness: 1 mm
  • Secure fixation via screws under the desktop
  • Available widths: 310 mm, 520 mm, 1000 mm
  • Available colours: Black, Grey, White
