EXIT R Cable Outlet

The EXIT R cable outlet is a practical and stylish solution for efficient cable management. Constructed from durable Zamak, it features a round design with a brush seal and is available in two diameters: Ø60 mm and Ø80 mm. Each size comes in two height options, 12 mm and 21 mm, allowing you to choose the perfect fit for your workspace.

The outlet is available in multiple finishes, including stainless steel effect, matte chrome, polished chrome, matte titanium, and anthracite, blending seamlessly with any interior style. Its twist-open lid mechanism ensures easy and convenient access to cables while maintaining a clean, professional look.

Ideal for desks, conference tables, or workstations, the EXIT R cable outlet keeps cables organized, reduces clutter, and enhances the overall aesthetic of your workspace.

Key Features:

  • Durable Zamak construction
  • Round design with brush seal
  • Available diameters: Ø60 mm and Ø80 mm
  • Available heights: 12 mm and 21 mm
  • Multiple finishes: stainless steel effect, matte chrome, polished chrome, matte titanium, anthracite
  • Easy twist-open lid mechanism
  • Modern and sleek design for professional workspaces
  • Ideal for desks, conference tables, and workstations
