EXIT G11 Eco Cable Outlet

The EXIT G11 Eco is a sleek and practical aluminum cable outlet designed to keep your workspace organized and clutter-free. Featuring a straight flip lid, it allows easy access to cables while maintaining a clean, professional look. Available with black or gray brush seals, it ensures tidy cable management without scratching or damaging cables.

This cable outlet comes in multiple anodized finishes, including matte white, titanium, and black, to match any interior style. With four size options—100x100x16 mm, 100x240x16 mm, 100x320x16 mm, and 100x450x16 mm—it provides flexible solutions for both small and larger cable arrangements.

Constructed from durable aluminum, the EXIT G11 Eco combines long-lasting performance with a minimalist design, making it ideal for office desks, conference tables, and other work surfaces.

Key Features:

  • Aluminum construction for durability
  • Square design with straight flip lid
  • Available with black or gray brush seals
  • Four anodized finishes: matte white, titanium, black, and aluminum
  • Four size options: 100x100x16 mm, 100x240x16 mm, 100x320x16 mm, 100x450x16 mm
  • Neat and practical cable management solution
  • Minimalist design suitable for desks and tables
