EXIT NCT Cable Outlet

The EXIT NCT is an elegant aluminum cable outlet featuring a high-quality brush seal, ideal for professional and residential environments alike. Its refined design makes it particularly suitable for TV setups, conference rooms, offices, and hotels — anywhere that requires clean and organized cable management.

Crafted from anodized aluminum, the EXIT NCT combines functionality with sophistication. Its elongated form allows for efficient storage and routing of multiple cables while maintaining a sleek, modern appearance. Available in various lengths, it adapts seamlessly to different furniture configurations and design requirements.

Key Features:

  • Elegant anodized aluminum construction
  • Integrated brush seal for neat cable routing
  • Suitable for offices, hotels, and home TV setups
  • Provides ample space for multiple cables
  • Modern, minimalist design for a refined aesthetic
  • Available in multiple sizes:
    • 200x45x20 mm
    • 403x69x20 mm
    • 427x45x20 mm
    • 450x45x20 mm
