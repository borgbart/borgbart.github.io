EXIT NCS Cable Outlet

The EXIT NCS is a long, narrow aluminum cable outlet designed for efficient and discreet cable management. Its rectangular form features brushed sides for a refined look and seamless integration into desks, conference tables, or workstations.

Constructed from durable aluminum and finished in black, the EXIT NCS is available in three lengths — 200 mm, 400 mm, and 800 mm — to suit a variety of table sizes and setups. Its low-profile design ensures minimal disruption while keeping cables neatly routed and accessible.

Key Features:

  • Durable black aluminum construction
  • Rectangular, long and narrow design
  • Brushed side finish for a premium look
  • Available lengths: 200 mm, 400 mm, 800 mm
  • Low-profile design for discreet cable management
  • Ideal for desks, conference tables, and professional workspaces
