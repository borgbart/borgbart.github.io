EXIT Duo Plus Cable Outlet

The EXIT Duo Plus is a premium dual aluminum cable outlet designed for two-sided access and smooth functionality. Featuring a straight flip lid with a soft-close mechanism, it ensures effortless and silent operation—perfect for shared desks and conference tables.

Equipped with a black brush seal for clean cable routing, the EXIT Duo Plus combines practicality with an elegant design. Available in four color finishes—black, anodized aluminum, titanium, and white—it complements a variety of interior styles. The outlet is offered in two sizes, 135x320x26 mm and 135x600x26 mm, providing flexibility for different table configurations.

Crafted from high-quality aluminum, this model delivers durability, precision, and a refined aesthetic, making it a perfect choice for modern office environments.

Key Features:

  • Dual-sided access for shared workspaces
  • Straight flip lid with soft-close mechanism
  • Black brush seal for clean cable routing
  • Durable aluminum construction
  • Available finishes: black, anodized, titanium, and white
  • Sizes: 135x320x26 mm and 135x600x26 mm
  • Elegant and functional design for conference tables and desks
