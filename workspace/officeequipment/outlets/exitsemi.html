EXIT Semi Cable Outlet

The EXIT Semi is a rectangular aluminum cable outlet designed for seamless integration into your worktop. Its unique construction allows a piece of the same surface material to be fitted onto the folding lid, creating a perfectly flush and discreet appearance that blends effortlessly with the table design.

Crafted from durable aluminum with a sleek silver finish, the EXIT Semi combines practicality with aesthetic refinement. Ideal for premium workstations and conference tables, it provides efficient cable management without compromising on design harmony.

With dimensions of 450x120x25 mm, this outlet delivers both functionality and elegance, making it an excellent choice for modern interiors seeking a clean, minimalist look.

Key Features:

  • Rectangular aluminum design with folding lid
  • Compatible with inlaid surface material for seamless integration
  • Durable silver aluminum construction
  • Flush finish for a clean, minimalist aesthetic
  • Ideal for workstations and conference tables
  • Dimensions: 450x120x25 mm
