EXIT Edge 16 Cable Outlet

The EXIT Edge 16 is a sleek aluminum cable outlet designed for clean and efficient cable management. Featuring a rounded flip lid with a black brush seal, it allows cables to pass through smoothly while maintaining a tidy and professional appearance.

Available in both square and rectangular shapes, the EXIT Edge 16 fits a variety of table configurations. With widths of 80 mm, 160 mm, and 240 mm, and a height of 16 mm, it provides versatile installation options for different tabletop thicknesses.

Constructed from high-quality aluminum, this cable outlet offers long-lasting durability and a refined, modern look—perfect for desks, conference tables, and collaborative workspaces.

Key Features:

  • Durable aluminum construction
  • Rounded flip lid with black brush seal
  • Available shapes: square and rectangular
  • Widths: 80 mm, 160 mm, and 240 mm
  • Height: 16 mm
  • Smooth cable routing with soft rounded edges
  • Elegant and minimalistic design
  • Ideal for desks, conference tables, and shared workspaces
