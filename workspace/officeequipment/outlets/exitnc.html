EXIT NC Cable Outlet

The EXIT NC is a sleek aluminum cable outlet designed for discreet and efficient cable management. Its minimalist rectangular or square design blends seamlessly into modern office, conference, and home environments, providing both style and practicality.

Made from durable aluminum, the EXIT NC features a clean, brushed finish and is available in three color variants — black, grey, and white. It is offered in three sizes: 100 x 100 x 20 mm (square), 100 x 45 x 20 (rectangular) and 180 x 45 x 20 mm (rectangular), allowing flexibility to suit various table and furniture configurations.

Key Features:

  • Premium aluminum construction
  • Available in black, grey, and white finishes
  • Square (69 x 69 x 20 mm) and rectangular (100 x 45 x 20) or (180 x 45 x 20 mm) versions
  • Minimalist design for modern interiors
  • Ideal for office, conference, and home settings
  • Durable and easy to install
