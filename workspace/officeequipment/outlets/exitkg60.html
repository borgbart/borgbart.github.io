EXIT KG Cable Outlet

The EXIT KG cable outlet is a practical and versatile solution for tidy cable management. Featuring a round Ø60 mm design with a flexible, star-shaped opening, it allows cables to pass through easily while keeping your workspace organized.

Constructed from durable plastic, the EXIT KG has a compact height of 25 mm and is available in three color options: Graphite Black (RAL 9011), White Aluminium, and Signal White. Its minimalist design ensures seamless integration with desks, conference tables, or workstations.

Easy to install and highly functional, the EXIT KG is ideal for managing power and data cables in both professional and home office environments.

Key Features:

  • Durable plastic construction
  • Round design with flexible, star-shaped opening
  • Diameter: Ø60 mm
  • Height: 25 mm
  • Available in three colors: Graphite Black, White Aluminium, Signal White
  • Compact and low-profile design
  • Ideal for desks, conference tables, and workstations
