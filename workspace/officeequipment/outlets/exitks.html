EXIT KS Cable Outlet

The EXIT KS cable outlet is a practical solution for tidy and organized cable management. Its square design features a small central rectangular opening, allowing cables to pass through neatly while maintaining a clean and professional look.

Constructed from durable plastic, the outlet measures 95x95x26 mm (WxDxH) and is available in three stylish finishes: Anthracite, Grey, and White. Its low-profile design ensures it integrates seamlessly into desks, conference tables, or workstations.

Easy to install and use, the EXIT KS cable outlet is ideal for managing power and data cables in both office and home environments, providing a sleek and functional cable management solution.

Key Features:

  • Durable plastic construction
  • Square design with central rectangular cable exit
  • Compact dimensions: 95x95x26 mm (WxDxH)
  • Available finishes: Anthracite, Grey, White
  • Low-profile design for seamless integration
  • Ideal for desks, conference tables, and workstations
  • Neat and practical cable management solution
