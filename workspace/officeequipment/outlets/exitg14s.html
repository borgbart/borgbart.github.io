EXIT G14 Series Cable Outlets

The EXIT G14 series aluminum cable outlets provide a sleek, practical, and durable solution for organized cable management. Featuring a soft-close mechanism and a flip lid with a black brush seal, these outlets combine functionality with a modern design, making them ideal for desks, conference tables, and workstations.

All G14 models come with a 14 mm radius in the drill holes and are available in four color finishes: Black, Aluminium, Titanium, and White. The series includes multiple sizes and shapes to suit different workspace requirements:

  • Square: 120x120x25 mm
  • Rectangular: 120x240x25 mm, 120x320x25 mm, 120x360x25 mm, 289.5x120x25 mm

These outlets are engineered to provide smooth, easy access to cables while keeping your workspace neat and clutter-free. Their robust aluminum construction ensures long-term durability, and the flip lid keeps cables concealed when not in use.

Key Features:

  • Durable aluminum construction
  • Soft-close flip lid with black brush seal
  • 14 mm radius drill holes for clean installation
  • Multiple finishes: Black, Aluminium, Titanium, White
  • Multiple sizes: 120x120x25 mm, 120x240x25 mm, 120x320x25 mm, 120x360x25 mm, 289.5x120x25 mm
  • Square and rectangular designs for flexible installation
  • Neat and functional cable management solution
  • Ideal for desks, conference tables, and workstations
