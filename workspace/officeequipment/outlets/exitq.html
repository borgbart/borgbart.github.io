EXIT Q Cable Outlet

The EXIT Q cable outlet combines functionality and modern design for efficient cable management. Featuring a round Ø80 mm opening within a square frame, it allows cables to pass through neatly while maintaining a sleek, professional appearance.

Constructed from durable Zamak material, the EXIT Q is designed to last and withstand daily use. The outlet is available in six finishes, including polished chrome and stainless steel effect, allowing it to match a variety of interior styles. Its simple twist mechanism makes opening and closing effortless, providing both convenience and style for your workspace.

Ideal for office desks, conference tables, or workstations, the EXIT Q cable outlet ensures tidy cable routing while adding a modern touch to your environment.

Key Features:

  • Durable Zamak construction
  • Round Ø80 mm cable opening in a square frame
  • Six finishes, including polished chrome and stainless steel effect
  • Easy twist-open mechanism for convenience
  • Modern and sleek design for professional workspaces
  • Suitable for desks, conference tables, and workstations
  • Compact dimensions: 89.5x23x89.5 mm (WxHxD)
