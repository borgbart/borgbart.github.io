Twee

Design: Massimo Cavana

An irregular handle takes shape, different in every view. Beside a strong formal character, this handle has an ergonomic grip, which contrasts with its angular and multifaceted image. Twee is available in satin or polished gold and satin or polished chrome finishes.

Available in the following finishes:

  • Graphite
  • Polished Chrome
  • Polished Brass
  • Satin Chrome
  • Satin Brass
  • Satin Nickel
