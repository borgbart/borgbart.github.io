Pivot Doors

The folding door system that combines stability and elegance.

An innovative hinge allows the door leaf to move parallel to the wall, offering both 180° and traditional 90° openings with ergonomic, stable, and silent movement. The door closes against jambs on three sides, ensuring excellent soundproofing. Its reduced clearance needs free up space next to the opening, while installation requires no masonry work. A versatile solution for interiors, walk-in wardrobes, and storage rooms, combining fluid movement, space efficiency, and refined functionality.

If you're interested in ordering a custom door, simply contact us and we'll connect you with one of our trusted professional carpenters. They will handle the design, manufacturing, and installation of your door to your exact specifications. We supply the premium opening system and hinge mechanism used in your door, ensuring smooth operation and long-lasting performance. The choice of door style, colour, and finish will be discussed directly between you and the carpenter, allowing full customization to your preferences.
