Folding Doors 90°

The folding system that redefines door movement.

This innovative system allows for both 90° and 180° folding openings using identical leaves and frames. The absence of a sliding track and the use of a traditional handle make it a unique and elegant solution. A true evolution in door design, combining versatility, comfort, and refined functionality for modern interiors.
