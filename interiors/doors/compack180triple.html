Pivot Doors

The triple-leaf folding system that maximises living space.

A patented hinge allows each leaf to fold laterally against the wall, reducing the clearance needs to just one third of the doorway. The movement is stable, silent, and ergonomic, while three-sided jambs provide excellent soundproofing. Easy to install and compatible with standard frames, it’s an ideal solution for private and public indoor spaces.
